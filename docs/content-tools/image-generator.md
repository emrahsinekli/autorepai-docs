# AI Image Generator <span className="badge badge--free">FREE</span>

**Auto RepAI → Image Generator** turns a text prompt into ready-to-use images and saves them straight into your media library.


![The AI Image Generator screen](/img/screenshots/image-generator.png)
## Step by step

1. **Describe the image.** Concrete beats abstract: *"top-down photo of a wooden desk with a laptop, coffee and a notebook, soft morning light"*.
2. Pick a **model** — GPT Image variants in free; [Pro](/pro/providers) adds Stable Diffusion 4 Ultra, Flux 2 (Max/Pro/Flex), Leonardo Phoenix and Gemini image models to the same screen.
3. Choose **size**, **style** (realistic, digital art, illustration, minimalist, artistic) and **count** (1–6).
4. Optional: upload a **reference image** to guide the composition (uses OpenAI's image-edit endpoint).
5. Click **Generate Image**, then **Save to Media Library** on the results you like.

## Prompt defaults

The **Set as Default** panel stores your house style — artist direction, lighting, camera, composition, resolution, color notes — and applies it to every future prompt so your images stay on-brand.

## Where images get used

- As **featured images** from the Content Generator's featured-image switch.
- Pro [Automations](/pro/automations) can attach AI images — or royalty-free stock photos (Pexels/Pixabay with your own free keys) — to scheduled posts automatically.

:::tip Alt text matters
After inserting images, run the [Image Alt Text Scanner](/woocommerce/image-alt-scanner) — images without alt text hurt accessibility and image SEO.
:::
