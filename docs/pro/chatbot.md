# AI Chatbot <span className="badge badge--pro">PRO</span>

The Pro chatbot answers visitors **from your own content**, captures leads, opens support tickets, and lets a human take over the conversation in real time.


![Chatbot administration](/img/screenshots/chatbot.png)
## Set it up

1. Go to **Auto RepAI → AI Chatbot → Settings**.
2. Write the bot's **role and rules** (system prompt), pick the provider/model, set the welcome message and widget colors/position.
3. Fill your [Knowledge Base](/pro/knowledge-base) — the bot grounds every answer in it and **refuses to invent** prices, policies or shipping details it doesn't know.
4. Enable the widget site-wide, or embed it inline on a page with the chat shortcode.

## Leads and tickets

- If a visitor types an **email address**, it's saved as a lead automatically (with the conversation attached) and you're notified.
- The **"talk to a human"** flow opens a support ticket with a reference number (e.g. `TKT-20260803-6E1F3`) and the full transcript; duplicate submissions are throttled.
- Everything lands in [Lead Management](/pro/lead-management).

## Live agent takeover

When a visitor asks for a human, the conversation appears under **AI Chatbot → Conversations → 🔴 Live conversations**. Reply from your dashboard and the visitor sees your messages in the widget within seconds — a real-time human handoff without any external live-chat service.

## WhatsApp hand-off

Add your WhatsApp number in settings and the handoff panel shows a green **"Continue on WhatsApp"** button (the visitor initiates the chat — you never message first).

## Cost & abuse protection

- **Daily budget** — when the bot's daily AI spend cap is hit, visitors get your friendly offline message + the contact form instead of an AI answer, and you're emailed once.
- **Rate limiting** per IP, same-origin checks, honeypot — bots and abuse can't burn your credits.
- Off means off: disabling the chatbot removes the widget and its assets entirely from the site.

## Privacy

Conversations are stored on **your** site (and are covered by the plugin's GDPR export/erase integration). Visitor messages go to your AI provider only — see [External services](/reference/external-services).
