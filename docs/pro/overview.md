# Auto RepAI Pro <span className="badge badge--pro">PRO</span>

The free plugin is a complete toolkit. **Pro turns it into a system that works for you** — repairing, publishing, answering and reporting on a schedule, while you keep full control of costs.

## Start a 7-day free trial

1. In WordPress, open **Auto RepAI → Pro Features** (or **Upgrade**).
2. Start the **7-day free trial** — no credit card required.
3. The Professional build installs as an in-place upgrade (same menu, same settings). Cancel any time; your free plugin keeps working exactly as before.

Pro is delivered and licensed through **Freemius**; one license can cover multiple sites depending on your plan.

## What you unlock

**Hands-free maintenance**
- [Autopilots](/pro/autopilots) — GEO fixer, accessibility repair, WooCommerce catalog rewriting and a weekly email report.
- [Automations](/pro/automations) — scheduled content creation from topic lists, RSS or URLs; content refresh; automatic internal linking; comment replies.

**Visitor-facing AI**
- [AI Chatbot](/pro/chatbot) — grounded in your [Knowledge Base](/pro/knowledge-base), with lead capture, support tickets, live agent takeover and WhatsApp hand-off.
- [AI Forms](/pro/ai-forms) — no-code AI tools for visitors (estimators, support helpers, brief generators).
- [AI Product Advisor](/pro/product-advisor) — visitors describe a need, your AI recommends matching products.

**Agency deliverables**
- [White-label & EAA reports](/pro/reports-white-label), the GEO trend chart, and [settings export/import](/pro/usage-limits#settings-exportimport) to roll your configuration across client sites.

**Content power tools**
- [Importers](/pro/importers) — YouTube-to-blog, audio-to-blog, Reddit research, Multi-Source, AI Notebook.
- [Internal Linker](/pro/internal-linker), [SEO Analyzer & Brand Monitor & Citation Check](/pro/analysis-tools), [Workflow Studio](/pro/workflow-studio), [Lead Management](/pro/lead-management).

**Providers & governance**
- [7 more AI providers](/pro/providers): Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Stability AI, Replicate, Leonardo.
- [Usage limits, webhooks and cost controls](/pro/usage-limits).

:::info Guideline-friendly by design
The free plugin on WordPress.org is complete and uncapped — Pro features are **separate additions**, not unlocks. That's why upgrading never changes how your free tools behave.
:::
