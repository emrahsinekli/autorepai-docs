# White-label & EAA reports <span className="badge badge--pro">PRO</span>

The free [Health Report](/content-tools/health-report) becomes an **agency deliverable** in Pro: branded, deeper, and ready to send to clients.


![White-label branding and report options](/img/screenshots/health-report.png)
## Set your branding once

On **Auto RepAI → White-label Report**:

1. Enter your **agency name**, **logo URL** (upload to the media library and paste its address), **accent color** and **report footer** (e.g. *"Prepared by Acme Digital — hello@acme.agency"*).
2. Click **Save branding**.

The printed report carries **only your brand** — no plugin branding anywhere on the sheet. Branding travels with [settings export/import](/pro/usage-limits#settings-exportimport), so you configure it once and roll it to every client site.

## Report types

**Website health (standard)** — the free report plus:
- **AI repair & content activity** for the chosen period (7/30/90 days), with an optional **AI spend** section (off by default — keep it off for client copies).
- The **28-day GEO score change** card from the [trend recorder](/pro/autopilots#geo-trend-recording).

**EAA accessibility deep-dive** — a compliance-shaped report:
- Findings mapped to **WCAG 2.1 success criteria** (1.1.1, 1.3.1, 2.4.4, 4.1.2) with affected pages, instance counts and a plain-language **remediation recommendation** per finding.
- **Site-level checks** (document language, 3.1.1) with pass/fail.
- **Per-page findings** for every reviewed page.
- An explicit **out-of-scope** section (contrast, keyboard, forms, media, third-party widgets) and the statement that this is an automated content review, **not a legal conformity assessment** — so your deliverable never overpromises.

## Generate and deliver

Pick the type, client name and period → **Generate report** → **Print / Save as PDF** (the print view contains only the branded sheet). Attach the PDF to your monthly client email — the GEO trend card does the "was it worth it?" conversation for you.
