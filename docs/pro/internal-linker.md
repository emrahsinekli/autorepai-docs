# AI Internal Linker <span className="badge badge--pro">PRO</span>

Internal links are the cheapest SEO you're not doing. The Internal Linker finds the opportunities, writes the anchor and places the link — you just approve.


![The Internal Linker with the applied-links archive](/img/screenshots/internal-linker.png)
## Manual flow

1. Go to **Auto RepAI → Internal Linker**.
2. Select the posts to scan and the AI model, then click **AI Scan for Links**.
3. Review the suggestions: each shows the **source post**, the proposed **anchor text** (real text already in the post) and the **target post**, with context.
4. **Apply** the ones you like (or dismiss). The link is inserted exactly at the anchor — the post's wording doesn't change. Already-linked text is never double-linked.

## Fully automatic <span className="badge badge--pro">via Automations</span>

Add an **Internal linking** task in [Automations](/pro/automations) and new content gets linked into your existing posts hands-free — the AI picks the anchor and target on a schedule.

## Applied links archive

Every applied link — manual or automatic — is recorded in the **Applied links archive** at the bottom of the page: date, post, anchor and target (last 300). It's your audit trail for "what changed on the site", and clicking through makes spot-checking easy.

:::tip Strategy
Point links **toward** your money pages (services, cornerstone guides, key products). After a Bulk Content batch, run one scan across the new drafts so they weave into your site from day one.
:::
