# Importers <span className="badge badge--pro">PRO</span>

Five Pro tools turn *other media* into WordPress articles.

## YouTube to Blog

1. **Auto RepAI → YouTube to Blog**, paste one or more YouTube URLs (one per line).
2. The transcript is fetched and rewritten into a structured article — not a raw transcript dump.
3. Review and create the draft. Great for repurposing your own channel into search traffic.

## Audio to Blog

Upload a podcast episode or recording (MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG). Whisper transcribes it, then the article is written from the transcript. Interviews become Q&A posts; solo episodes become how-tos.

## Reddit Research

Enter a topic or a subreddit: Auto RepAI pulls the top discussions and writes an article informed by what real people actually ask and argue about — excellent for pain-point content. Add free [Reddit app credentials](/pro/providers#reddit-credentials) so it works reliably from server IPs.

## Multi-Source Generator

Combine up to several sources — URLs, YouTube videos, Reddit threads, pasted text — into **one** coherent article with a title and focus you set. The strongest tool for roundups and comparison pieces.

## AI Notebook

Paste notes, import a Google Docs share link or any URL into the notebook; then ask the AI to process the material — summarize, outline, or draft a post from it — and publish the result. Think of it as a scratchpad with a writer attached.

:::tip Attribution
When your article leans on specific sources, cite and link them. The importers give you a draft grounded in the material — editorial judgment (and credit) stays with you.
:::
