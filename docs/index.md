# AISiteVolt Documentation

**AISiteVolt doesn't just generate content — it repairs it.** It fixes your old posts for Google, makes your articles quotable by ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini (GEO), repairs thin WooCommerce product pages, and scores your content against accessibility standards — all inside your WordPress dashboard, with **your own OpenAI API key**. No monthly content fees, no data through our servers.

## What's in the free plugin?

Everything you need to publish AI-assisted content, with no usage caps:

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Content Generator](/content-tools/content-generator) | SEO-friendly blog posts with any OpenAI model |
| [AI Image Generator](/content-tools/image-generator) | Featured images with GPT Image |
| [Content Repair](/content-tools/auto-repair) | Fix grammar, SEO, readability and outdated info in existing posts |
| [Quick AI Edit](/content-tools/quick-ai-edit) | Rewrite, expand or translate selected text right in the editor |
| [Bulk Content](/content-tools/bulk-content) | Turn a topic list into queued drafts with SEO metadata |
| [AI Visibility analyzer](/seo-geo/ai-visibility) | GEO score for every post + an AI-visibility column in the posts list |
| [llms.txt generator](/seo-geo/llms-txt) | Introduce your site to AI crawlers in one click |
| [WooCommerce scanner](/woocommerce/product-scanner) | Find products with thin descriptions and missing SEO meta |
| [Image alt text scanner](/woocommerce/image-alt-scanner) | Find media images with no alt text |
| [Accessibility scanner](/accessibility/accessibility-scanner) | WCAG / EAA content findings with per-post scores |
| [Health Report](/content-tools/health-report) | Printable website health report (GEO + accessibility) |

## What does Pro add?

[AISiteVolt Pro](/pro/overview) turns the toolkit into a system that works for you: **autopilots** repair your weakest posts, products and accessibility issues on a schedule; an **AI chatbot** answers visitors from your own Knowledge Base and captures leads; **Automations** create and refresh content from topic lists, RSS and URLs; **white-label reports** let agencies send branded PDF health reports to clients; plus 7 more AI providers (Claude, Gemini, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Stability, Replicate, Leonardo). Every Pro purchase starts with a **7-day free trial**.

:::tip Your data stays yours
AISiteVolt is a **bring-your-own-key** plugin. Prompts go directly from your server to your AI provider. Nothing passes through AISiteVolt servers — see [External services & privacy](/reference/external-services).
:::
