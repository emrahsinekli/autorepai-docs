# Frequently asked questions

### Is the free plugin really free — no caps, no trial?

Yes. Every free feature is complete and uncapped. You pay only your AI provider (OpenAI) for the tokens you use. Pro is a **separate** set of additional modules, not an unlock.

### How much does a blog post cost me in API fees?

Typically a few cents with GPT-4o (less with GPT-4o Mini). Every run shows its exact token usage and estimated cost, and batch actions show an estimate **before** running.

### Does my content pass through Auto RepAI's servers?

No. Prompts go **directly from your server to your AI provider**, billed to your own key. See [External services](/reference/external-services).

### Which AI models can I use for free?

Every OpenAI text and image model — GPT-4o, the GPT-5.x family, GPT Image. OpenAI models are never restricted in the free plugin. [Pro](/pro/providers) adds Claude, Gemini, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Stability, Replicate and Leonardo.

### Will AI content hurt my SEO?

Unedited, mass-published AI content can. Auto RepAI's philosophy is **repair + review**: ground generation in your facts, repair what exists, and always edit drafts. Google's guidance targets unhelpful content, not AI assistance per se.

### What is GEO and why should I care?

Generative Engine Optimization — being quotable by ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini answers. A growing share of discovery happens there. Start with the [AI Visibility scanner](/seo-geo/ai-visibility) and [llms.txt](/seo-geo/llms-txt).

### Does it work with my page builder / theme?

Generated content is standard WordPress post content, so yes. Quick AI Edit works in Gutenberg and Classic; page-builder-specific layouts are best edited in the builder after generation.

### Do I need WooCommerce?

Only for the store tools ([product scanner](/woocommerce/product-scanner), [alt-text scanner](/woocommerce/image-alt-scanner), [Product Advisor](/pro/product-advisor)). Everything else works on any WordPress site.

### How does the Pro trial work?

7 days, no credit card, started from the plugin (delivered via Freemius). Cancel and the free plugin continues untouched.

### Can I use one Pro license on client sites?

Multi-site licensing depends on the plan you pick at checkout. [Settings export/import](/pro/usage-limits#settings-exportimport) makes rolling your configuration to each site trivial.

### What happens to my data if I uninstall?

Deleting the plugin removes all its tables, options, scheduled jobs and its own post meta. Your posts, products, and their content remain untouched.

### Where do I get support?

Email **support@autorepai.com**, or use the [WordPress.org support forum](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/auto-repai-ai-content-generator/) for the free plugin.
