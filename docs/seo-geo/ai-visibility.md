# AI Visibility (GEO) <span className="badge badge--free">FREE</span>

People increasingly ask **ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini** instead of searching. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is about making your content *quotable* by those AI answers. Auto RepAI scores exactly that.


![The AI Visibility (GEO) screen with the trend chart](/img/screenshots/ai-visibility.png)
## Scan your posts

1. Go to **Auto RepAI → AI Visibility**.
2. Click **Load Posts**, then **Scan** per post (or **Scan All**).
3. Each post gets a **0–100 score** with its missing pieces listed:
   - a quotable **direct-answer opening**,
   - an **FAQ section**,
   - **FAQ schema** (structured data),
   - scannable structure.

The score also appears as a **column in your posts list**, so you always see GEO health at a glance.

## Read the score

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 80–100 | Quotable — AI answers can lift a clean answer from the post |
| 50–79 | Partial — some elements missing |
| 0–49 | Invisible to AI answers — needs work |

## Fix with AI <span className="badge badge--pro">PRO</span>

Pro repairs a post in one click: it writes a 40–70-word **direct answer** at the top, appends a four-question **FAQ section** built from the post, and attaches **FAQPage schema** — then rescores. The [autopilot](/pro/autopilots) does this on a schedule for your weakest posts, and the **Citation Check** actually asks an AI assistant your post's question and reports whether your site gets cited.

## Track the trend <span className="badge badge--pro">PRO</span>

Pro records a **daily snapshot** of your site-wide GEO score and charts it on this page (with 7-day and 28-day deltas). The [white-label report](/pro/reports-white-label) includes the 28-day change — proof for clients that the work moves the needle.
