Auto RepAI Documentation

Auto RepAI doesn't just generate content — it repairs it. It fixes your old posts for Google, makes your articles quotable by ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini (GEO), repairs thin WooCommerce product pages, and scores your content against accessibility standards — all inside your WordPress dashboard, with your own OpenAI API key. No monthly content fees, no data through our servers.

🚀 InstallationInstall the free plugin from WordPress.org in two minutes.🔑 API keysConnect your own OpenAI key — the only setup you need.🖥️ Run it locallyUse Ollama or LM Studio on your own machine — no key, no cost, nothing leaves your server.✍️ First articleGenerate, review and publish your first AI-assisted post.🔧 Auto RepairLet AI fix grammar, SEO, readability and stale facts in existing posts.🤖 AI Visibility (GEO)Score and repair every post for AI-search citability.📊 Health ReportA printable GEO + accessibility report — free and unlimited.🛒 WooCommerce AIFind and rewrite thin product content in bulk.⭐ Auto RepAI ProAutopilots, chatbot, automations, white-label reports and more.

What does Pro add?​

Auto RepAI Pro turns the toolkit into a system that works for you: autopilots repair your weakest posts, products and accessibility issues on a schedule; an AI chatbot answers visitors from your own Knowledge Base and captures leads; Automations create and refresh content from topic lists, RSS and URLs; white-label reports let agencies send branded PDF health reports to clients; plus 7 more AI providers (Claude, Gemini, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Stability, Replicate, Leonardo). Every Pro purchase starts with a 7-day free trial.

Your data stays yours

Auto RepAI is a bring-your-own-key plugin. Prompts go directly from your server to your AI provider. Nothing passes through Auto RepAI servers — see External services & privacy.
